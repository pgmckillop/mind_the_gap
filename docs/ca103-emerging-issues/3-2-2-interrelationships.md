## Interrelationships between digital and emerging technologies

This section covers how digital and emerging technologies interrelate and how these combined developments impact organisations, society and individuals in digital software development.

---

## Interrelationships between technologies

**Features / Characteristics**
- Digital systems provide the platforms, data and connectivity that enable emerging technologies  
- Emerging technologies depend on existing digital infrastructure, including networks, storage and processing  
- Technologies evolve together, with advances in one area accelerating development in others  
- Shared reliance on data, automation and connectivity

**Benefits**
- Accelerated innovation across multiple sectors  
- Increased automation and efficiency  
- Expansion of digital services and capabilities  
- Creation of new roles and technical specialisms  

**Drawbacks**
- Increased system complexity  
- Greater dependence on interconnected infrastructure  
- Wider attack surface for security threats  
- Rapid pace of change creating skills gaps  

---

## Impact on organisations

**Features / Characteristics**
- Integration of emerging technologies into existing digital systems  
- Increased reliance on data-driven and automated processes  

**Benefits**
- Improved productivity and operational efficiency  
- Enhanced decision-making through advanced analytics  
- Opportunity to develop new products and services  

**Drawbacks**
- High implementation and maintenance costs  
- Requirement for specialist skills  
- Increased risk exposure from interconnected systems  

---

## Impact on society

**Features / Characteristics**
- Widespread adoption of digitally enabled emerging technologies  
- Expansion of online services and automated systems  

**Benefits**
- Improved access to information and services  
- Greater global connectivity  
- New employment sectors  

**Drawbacks**
- Job displacement  
- Digital exclusion  
- Privacy and surveillance concerns  

---

## Impact on individuals

**Features / Characteristics**
- Increased interaction with automated and intelligent systems  
- Growing dependence on digital platforms  

**Benefits**
- Enhanced convenience and access to services  
- New learning and employment opportunities  

**Drawbacks**
- Changing skill requirements  
- Reduced human interaction  
- Potential loss of autonomy in decision-making  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of combined digital and emerging technologies depends on:

i. organisational readiness and availability of technical skills  
ii. cost of adoption and ongoing management  
iii. robustness of data governance and security controls  
iv. societal capacity to adapt to changing employment and service models  
v. effectiveness of measures to support inclusion and mitigate privacy risks  

Interrelated technologies drive innovation and efficiency but increase complexity and dependency. Their impact is constrained by infrastructure, workforce capability, regulatory considerations and social adaptation. Balanced implementation is required to realise benefits while managing risk and disruption.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Marks are awarded for explaining **how technologies work together and why that matters**. Link interrelationships to impacts on organisations, society and individuals, then evaluate suitability using constraints such as cost, skills, security and inclusion. Avoid listing technologies without analysing their combined effects.
