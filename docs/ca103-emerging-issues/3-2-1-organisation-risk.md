# 3.2.1 

## Developments in technologies and their impact

This section covers how developments in technologies impact organisations, individuals and society.

---

## Storage media

### Increased demand for storage

**Features / Characteristics**
- Growth in digital content, data collection and system logging
- Expansion of cloud and on-premise storage capacity

**Benefits**
- Enables retention of large datasets  
- Supports analytics and historical tracking  
- Facilitates digital services at scale  

**Drawbacks**
- Increased infrastructure and management costs  
- Higher energy consumption  
- Greater responsibility for data protection  

---

## Processing technologies

### Quantum computing

**Features / Characteristics**
- Extremely high-speed processing for specific problem types

**Benefits**
- Potential breakthroughs in optimisation and analysis  
- Accelerated complex computations  

**Drawbacks**
- Limited availability  
- High cost  
- Immature ecosystem and skills shortage  

---

## Internet of Things (IoT)

### Edge computing

**Features / Characteristics**
- Data processing performed close to data sources

**Benefits**
- Reduced latency  
- Lower bandwidth usage  
- Faster local decision making  

**Drawbacks**
- Increased device complexity  
- Distributed security risks  

---

### Use within different contexts (industrial, smart city, domestic)

**Features / Characteristics**
- Networked sensors and devices embedded into environments

**Benefits**
- Improved automation  
- Enhanced monitoring and control  
- Greater efficiency in services  

**Drawbacks**
- Expanded attack surface  
- Privacy concerns  
- Dependence on connectivity  

---

## Artificial intelligence

### Generative AI

**Features / Characteristics**
- Automated creation of text, images, video and audio

**Benefits**
- Increased productivity  
- Rapid content generation  
- Creative and analytical support  

**Drawbacks**
- Authenticity concerns  
- Risk of misinformation  
- Dependency on automated output  

---

### Machine learning

**Features / Characteristics**
- Systems learn patterns from data to improve outcomes

**Benefits**
- Improved prediction accuracy  
- Personalised services  
- Process optimisation  

**Drawbacks**

- Lack of transparency  
- Requirement for high-quality data

---

## Judgement

Suitability of emerging technologies depends on:

i. availability of skills and infrastructure  
ii. cost of implementation and maintenance  
iii. data quality and security controls  
iv. environmental impact  
v. organisational readiness and regulatory considerations  

Technological developments enable automation, efficiency and innovation but introduce risks relating to cost, sustainability, workforce displacement and data governance. Outcomes are constrained by technical maturity, resource availability and societal adaptation.
