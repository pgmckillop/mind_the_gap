# Content Area 3: Emerging Issues

Students will explore the impact of digital and emerging technologies on organisations, individuals and society, considering how increased reliance on digital systems influences ways of working, communication, behaviour and access to services.

Students will examine the importance of digital inclusion and understand how end user characteristics, such as age, skills, culture and additional needs, affect the use of and access to digital systems. This includes recognising barriers to inclusion and the role of accessibility and best practice guidelines.

Students will also investigate emerging technologies and their implications, evaluating how technological developments influence organisational practice, societal change and individual experience. Judgements will be made about these impacts within the context of digital software development.
