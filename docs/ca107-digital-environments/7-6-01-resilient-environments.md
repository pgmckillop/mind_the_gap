## Benefits of resilient environments

This section covers the benefits of resilient environments and their impact on organisations and clients.

---

## Increased security

### Data security (storage and transfer)

**Features**

- Protection of data at rest and in transit  

**Benefits**

- Reduces risk of unauthorised access  
- Supports confidentiality and integrity of organisational and client data  

**Drawbacks**

- Increased security controls add complexity  
- May introduce performance overhead  

---

### Reduced vulnerabilities

**Features**

- Systems are designed to minimise exposure to threats  

**Benefits**

- Lowers likelihood of successful attacks  
- Improves overall system reliability  

**Drawbacks**

- Requires continuous monitoring and maintenance  

---

## Increased reputation

### Protect brand/image

**Features**

- Reliable and secure systems reflect professional practice  

**Benefits**

- Enhances organisational credibility  
- Demonstrates responsibility in handling data  

**Drawbacks**

- Reputation damage is significant if resilience measures fail  

---

### Retain customer confidence

**Features**

- Consistent service availability and data protection  

**Benefits**

- Builds trust with clients  
- Encourages continued use of services  

**Drawbacks**

- High expectations increase pressure to maintain resilience  

---

## Reduction in downtime

### Features

- Systems are designed to recover quickly from failures  

### Benefits

- Maintains business continuity  
- Reduces disruption to clients and operations  

### Drawbacks

- Implementing resilience increases cost and system complexity  

---

## Judgement

Resilient environments improve security, protect organisational reputation, and reduce downtime, benefiting both organisations and clients. However, resilience introduces additional cost, configuration effort, and ongoing maintenance.

Suitability depends on:

i. security requirements  
ii. tolerance to service interruption  
iii. reputational risk  
iv. available resources  

Balancing resilience against cost and complexity is essential when designing dependable systems.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Higher marks are gained by linking **resilience features** to **organisational and client impact** (for example reduced downtime leading to retained customer confidence). Avoid listing benefits without explaining their effects.
