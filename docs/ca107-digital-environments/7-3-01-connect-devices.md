## Benefits and drawbacks of connecting devices to form networks

This section considers the advantages and disadvantages of connecting devices together to form networks.

---

## Benefits

### Resource sharing

- Enables shared access to hardware, software, and data  

**Benefit**

- Reduces duplication of resources  
- Supports efficient use of storage and peripherals  

---

### Communication

- Allows devices and users to exchange data  

**Benefit**

- Supports collaboration and information exchange  
- Enables centralised services and distributed working  

---

### Centralised management

- Supports control of users, devices, and data from a single point  

**Benefit**

- Simplifies administration  
- Supports consistent configuration and maintenance  

---

### Data accessibility

- Enables access to shared data from multiple locations  

**Benefit**

- Improves availability of information  
- Supports coordinated working  

---

## Drawbacks

### Security risks

- Increased exposure to unauthorised access and malicious activity  

**Drawback**

- A single breach can affect multiple connected devices  

---

### Dependence on network availability

- Systems rely on network connectivity  

**Drawback**

- Network failure can disrupt access to services and data  

---

### Performance impact

- Multiple devices compete for bandwidth and shared resources  

**Drawback**

- Can result in slower response times during high demand  

---

### Setup and maintenance requirements

- Networks require configuration and ongoing management  

**Drawback**

- Increased administrative effort and technical expertise needed  

---

## Judgement

Connecting devices to form networks provides clear benefits through shared resources, communication, and centralised management. However, these advantages must be balanced against:

i. increased security exposure  
ii. reliance on network availability  
iii. potential performance degradation  
iv. added setup and maintenance demands  

Suitability depends on organisational needs, scale of use, required availability, and tolerance for risk and administrative overhead.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Marks are awarded for balanced responses that explain **both benefits and drawbacks**. Higher credit is gained by linking networking advantages (such as resource sharing) to constraints (such as security risks or dependence on connectivity).
