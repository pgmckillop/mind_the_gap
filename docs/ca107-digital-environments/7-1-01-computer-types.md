# 7.1.1 Understand the features and use of different types of physical computers

This section covers the features and use of personal computers, mobile devices, servers and embedded devices, and how each type supports different operational requirements in digital environments.

---

## Personal computers

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- general-purpose computing devices designed for individual users  
- support a wide range of applications and development tools  
- provide keyboard, mouse and display interfaces  
- typically operate using desktop operating systems  

**Benefits**

- flexible for development and productivity tasks  
- relatively low cost compared to enterprise systems  
- easy to upgrade and maintain  

**Drawbacks**

- limited scalability  
- dependent on local hardware performance  
- less suitable for continuous high-load processing  

---

## Mobile devices (smartphones and tablets)

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- portable computing devices with touch interfaces  
- use mobile operating systems  
- optimised for battery efficiency and wireless connectivity  
- include integrated sensors and cameras  

**Benefits**

- high portability and accessibility  
- support remote working and communication  
- enable context-aware applications through built-in sensors  

**Drawbacks**

- limited processing power compared to desktops and servers  
- constrained storage capacity  
- shorter device lifecycles  

---

## Servers

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- high-performance computers designed to provide services to multiple clients  
- operate continuously in networked environments  
- optimised for reliability, availability and scalability  
- commonly used for hosting applications, data and network services  

**Benefits**

- support large numbers of users  
- enable centralised data management  
- provide high availability and redundancy options  

**Drawbacks**

- higher acquisition and maintenance costs  
- require specialist management  
- increased energy consumption  

---

## Embedded devices

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- dedicated computing systems built into larger products  
- designed to perform specific tasks  
- operate with limited resources  
- commonly integrated with sensors and actuators  

**Benefits**

- efficient for specialised functions  
- low power consumption  
- support automation and real-time control  

**Drawbacks**

- limited flexibility  
- difficult to upgrade  
- constrained processing and storage capabilities  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of physical computer types in digital software development depends on:

i. performance requirements and workload characteristics  
ii. cost and resource availability  
iii. scalability and availability needs  
iv. operational context and deployment environment  

Personal computers provide flexible development platforms, mobile devices prioritise portability, servers support high-availability services, and embedded devices deliver specialised functionality. Selection is constrained by budget, performance demands and management overhead.
