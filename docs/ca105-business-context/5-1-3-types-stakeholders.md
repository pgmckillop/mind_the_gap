# 5.1.3 

## Types of stakeholders

This section covers the different types of stakeholders involved in organisations, grouped as internal and external stakeholders, and their roles in influencing organisational decisions and digital activity.

---

## Internal stakeholders

### Owners

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Provide capital and hold ultimate ownership  
- Define strategic direction and organisational objectives  

**Benefits**
- Clear authority over business priorities  
- Direct influence on investment decisions  

**Drawbacks**
- May prioritise financial return over operational or technical considerations  
- Limited day-to-day involvement in digital delivery  

---

### Directors

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Responsible for organisational governance and leadership  
- Translate ownership goals into operational strategy  

**Benefits**
- Provide oversight and accountability  
- Enable coordinated decision-making  

**Drawbacks**
- Strategic decisions may be disconnected from technical constraints  
- Change approval processes can slow implementation  

---

### Employees

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Deliver services or products  
- Operate and maintain digital systems  

**Benefits**
- Direct operational knowledge  
- Essential for successful adoption of digital change  

**Drawbacks**
- Resistance to change may occur  
- Skills gaps can limit effective system use  

---

## External stakeholders

### Customers/clients

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Receive products or services  
- Influence demand and usability requirements  

**Benefits**
- Provide revenue  
- Offer feedback that informs improvement  

**Drawbacks**
- Expectations may exceed organisational capability  
- Dissatisfaction can impact reputation  

---

### Suppliers

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Provide goods or services required for operation  

**Benefits**
- Enable continuity of business activities  
- May offer specialist expertise  

**Drawbacks**
- Dependency risks  
- Disruption can affect service delivery  

---

### Shareholders

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Invest capital in return for financial performance  

**Benefits**
- Provide funding for growth  
- Encourage organisational accountability  

**Drawbacks**
- May prioritise short-term returns  
- Limited involvement in operational detail  

---

### Outsourced services

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Deliver external services such as hosting, support, or development  

**Benefits**
- Access to specialist skills  
- Reduced internal staffing requirements  

**Drawbacks**
- Reduced direct control  
- Reliance on external performance  

---

### Investors/funders

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Provide financial resources for development or expansion  

**Benefits**
- Enable large-scale projects  
- Support innovation  

**Drawbacks**
- Influence over organisational direction  
- Expectations of measurable returns  

---

### Government

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Regulates organisational activity  
- May provide funding or contracts  

**Benefits**
- Provides legal frameworks  
- Can offer financial support  

**Drawbacks**
- Compliance requirements increase administrative burden  
- Policy changes can affect operational stability  

---

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. level of influence held by each stakeholder group  
ii. alignment between stakeholder expectations and organisational objectives  
iii. availability of financial and human resources  
iv. regulatory and governance constraints  
v. capacity to manage competing priorities  

Stakeholder impact varies by organisation type and sector. Digital software development must balance operational needs, funding pressures, regulatory requirements, and user expectations when making design and implementation decisions.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing stakeholders without explanation. Marks are awarded for linking stakeholder roles to organisational impact and suitability. Responses must show how stakeholder influence affects decisions and constraints, not just identify internal and external groups.
