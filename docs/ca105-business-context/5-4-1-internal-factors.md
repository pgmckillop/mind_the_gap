# 5.4.1 

## Internal factors that trigger change in organisations

This section covers the internal organisational factors that drive change, including structural adjustments, scale of operation, and shifts in strategic objectives.

---

## Organisational restructuring

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Reorganisation of departments, roles, or management structures  
- Realignment of responsibilities and workflows  

**Benefits**
- Improved operational efficiency  
- Clearer accountability  
- Better alignment with organisational goals  

**Drawbacks**
- Disruption to staff and processes  
- Temporary reduction in productivity  
- Resistance to change  

---

## Expansion

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Growth in workforce, services, or market reach  
- Increased demand on systems and infrastructure  

**Benefits**
- Increased revenue potential  
- Wider customer base  
- Greater organisational capacity  

**Drawbacks**
- Higher operational costs  
- Increased complexity in management and systems  
- Pressure on existing resources  

---

## Downsizing

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Reduction in staff numbers or operational scope  
- Often driven by financial or performance pressures  

**Benefits**
- Reduced costs  
- Streamlined operations  

**Drawbacks**
- Loss of skills and experience  
- Lower staff morale  
- Reduced service capability  

---

## New strategic objectives

### Diversification  
### Rebranding  
### Additional features or services  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Shift in organisational direction or market positioning  
- Introduction of new offerings or refreshed organisational identity  

**Benefits**
- Access to new markets  
- Improved competitiveness  
- Enhanced organisational relevance  

**Drawbacks**
- Requires investment in systems and training  
- Risk of unclear focus  
- Increased pressure on delivery timelines  

---

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. organisational readiness for change  
ii. availability of financial and human resources  
iii. impact on existing processes and systems  
iv. clarity of strategic objectives  
v. capacity to manage disruption  

Internal change drivers shape how digital systems are adapted or replaced. Decisions must balance growth or restructuring ambitions against constraints such as cost, staff capability, operational continuity, and system scalability.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing internal factors without explanation. Marks are gained by linking each factor to organisational impact and evaluating how restructuring, expansion, downsizing, or strategic change affects suitability and constraints.
