## Factors determining the feasibility of a digital project

This section covers the benefits and drawbacks, risks, and constraints that determine whether a digital project is feasible within an organisation.

---

## Benefits and drawbacks

### Financial savings  
### Cost of implementing change  
### Impact on processes, including productivity gains, improved communication and security  
### Provision of new products  
### Impact on company reputation  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Evaluates expected return versus investment  
- Considers operational improvement and market impact  

**Benefits**
- Potential reduction in long-term operating costs  
- Increased efficiency and productivity  
- Improved communication and security  
- Enables delivery of new products  
- Opportunity to enhance organisational reputation  

**Drawbacks**
- High upfront implementation costs  
- Delayed return on investment  
- Risk of reputational harm if project fails  

---

## Risks

### Resistance to change from workforce  
### Misuse of new systems  
### Inadequate support for new system  
### Inadequate knowledge of new system  
### Disruption caused by implementation of new systems  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Human and operational factors affecting adoption  

**Benefits**
- Early identification supports mitigation planning  

**Drawbacks**
- Reduced productivity  
- Increased error rates  
- Failure to realise intended benefits  

---

## Constraints

### Budget  
### Time  
### Human resources and technological resources  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Defines project boundaries and delivery capability  

**Benefits**
- Forces prioritisation and realistic planning  

**Drawbacks**
- Limits scope and system quality  
- May delay or prevent implementation  

---

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. balance between financial savings and implementation cost  
ii. level of organisational risk tolerance  
iii. workforce readiness and availability of support  
iv. impact on existing processes and reputation  
v. constraints of budget, time, and technical capacity  

Digital project feasibility is determined by whether anticipated benefits outweigh risks and constraints. Successful delivery requires alignment between organisational objectives, available resources, and the ability to manage disruption during implementation.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing benefits, risks, or constraints in isolation. Marks are awarded for linking feasibility factors to organisational impact and evaluating trade-offs between cost, risk, and resource limitations to justify suitability.
