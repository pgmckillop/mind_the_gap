## Change management process

This section covers the processes involved in managing organisational change, including governance, planning, implementation, documentation, and review, along with the associated benefits and drawbacks.

---

### Amendment to existing system

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. scale and complexity of change  
ii. effectiveness of governance and stakeholder engagement  
iii. availability of resources and technical capability  
iv. risk tolerance and business continuity requirements  
v. quality of testing, documentation, and rollback planning  

Change management enables controlled system evolution but introduces administrative effort and time overhead. Digital software development must balance speed of delivery with governance, resilience, and organisational constraints.
