## Use of digital systems to support key organisational areas

This section explains how digital systems are used to support core organisational functions, including sales and marketing, research, design and development, human resources, operations, management, logistics, and finance.

---

## Sales and marketing

### Better market research  
### Better brand promotion, including social media  
### Online selling  
### Contextualising customer behaviour to personalise services offered  
### Better customer retention  
### Brand differentiation and values  
### Use of analytic tools, including search and social media analytics  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Collects and analyses customer data  
- Supports online engagement and transactions  
- Enables targeted communication and personalisation  
- Uses analytics to measure reach and behaviour  

**Benefits**
- Improved understanding of customer preferences  
- Increased sales opportunities through online channels  
- Stronger brand visibility  
- Data-driven decision making  

**Drawbacks**
- Dependence on accurate data  
- Ongoing costs for platforms and analytics tools  
- Privacy and compliance risks

### Provision of unique products and services  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. organisational priorities and operational scale  
ii. availability of financial and technical resources  
iii. data quality and system reliability  
iv. regulatory and security constraints  
v. staff capability to adopt digital processes  

Digital systems provide efficiency, visibility, and automation across organisational areas, but effectiveness is constrained by cost, implementation complexity, data accuracy, and ongoing management requirements.
