## Impact of risks on organisations when using digital systems

This section covers the potential organisational impacts that can result from risks associated with the use of digital systems, including legal, financial, operational, and reputational consequences.

---

## Legal action

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Claims brought by customers, partners, or regulators  
- Triggered by breaches of security, privacy, or contractual obligations  

**Benefits**
- Encourages stronger compliance and governance practices  

**Drawbacks**
- High legal costs  
- Management distraction  
- Long-term operational disruption  

---

## Fines

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Financial penalties imposed by regulatory bodies  
- Often linked to non-compliance or data breaches  

**Benefits**
- Reinforces accountability  
- Drives improvement in organisational controls  

**Drawbacks**
- Immediate financial loss  
- Reduced budgets for development and innovation  

---

## Reputational damage

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Loss of public confidence and trust  
- Negative perception by customers and stakeholders  

**Benefits**
- Forces review of organisational practices and systems  

**Drawbacks**
- Reduced customer engagement  
- Difficulty attracting clients, partners, or staff  

---

## Withdrawal of licence to practise

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Regulatory bodies remove permission to operate  
- Occurs following serious or repeated failures  

**Benefits**
- Protects wider society and industry standards  

**Drawbacks**
- Complete loss of operational capability  
- Severe financial impact  
- Organisational closure risk  

---

## Loss of business

**Features / Characteristics / Role**
- Customers move to competitors  
- Contracts are terminated or not renewed  

**Benefits**
- Highlights need for service improvement  

**Drawbacks**
- Reduced revenue  
- Downsizing or redundancy  
- Long-term sustainability threatened  

---

## Judgement

Suitability depends on:

i. severity and frequency of digital risks  
ii. effectiveness of organisational controls  
iii. regulatory environment and compliance requirements  
iv. resilience of reputation and customer trust  
v. financial capacity to absorb losses  

The impact of digital risks extends beyond technical failure to legal standing, market position, and organisational survival. Managing these risks requires balancing innovation with compliance, reliability, and stakeholder confidence.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Marks are awarded for explaining impact, not just naming consequences. Link risks to outcomes such as legal action or reputational damage, then evaluate how these affect organisational viability to support judgement-based answers.
