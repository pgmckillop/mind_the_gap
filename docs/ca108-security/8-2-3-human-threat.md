## Understand potential human threats, including prevention and mitigation methods, to systems and data

This section covers human-related threats to systems and data, including accidental actions, deliberate misuse, external impersonation, and poor cyber hygiene, together with associated prevention and mitigation methods.

---

## Human error

Includes:

- file properties  
- confirmation boxes  
- staff training  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Occurs through accidental actions or lack of awareness  
- Often involves incorrect handling of files or confirmation of unintended actions  
- Typically mitigated through interface controls and training  

### Benefits

- None for organisations  

### Drawbacks

- Accidental data exposure or loss  
- System misconfiguration  
- Increased likelihood of security incidents  

---

## Malicious employee

Includes:

- immediate removal from premises  
- suspend user accounts immediately  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Involves intentional misuse of authorised access  
- High risk due to existing system privileges and knowledge  

### Benefits

- None for organisations  

### Drawbacks

- Insider access enables rapid data theft or system damage  
- Difficult to detect until harm has occurred  

---

## Disguised criminal

Includes:

- accompany all visitors  
- check identification of visitors  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Individuals gain physical or digital access by impersonating legitimate users  
- Exploits trust and weak visitor controls  

### Benefits

- None for organisations  

### Drawbacks

- Unauthorised access to systems or secure areas  
- Increased risk of data compromise or sabotage  

---

## Poor cyber hygiene

Includes:

- locking all unattended machines  
- not writing down passwords  
- poor password management  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Results from weak everyday security practices  
- Creates opportunities for unauthorised access  

### Benefits

- Convenience for users  

### Drawbacks

- Compromised credentials  
- Increased exposure to internal and external threats  
- Weakens overall security posture  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of prevention and mitigation depends on:

i. staff awareness and training provision  
ii. effectiveness of access controls and monitoring  
iii. organisational constraints such as time, cost and management oversight  

Human threats are often less predictable than technical vulnerabilities and can bypass automated controls. While training, procedural safeguards and rapid account suspension reduce risk, they require continuous reinforcement and management commitment. Effective mitigation balances usability with behavioural controls and operational oversight.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing threats alone. Link *human behaviour → security weakness → organisational impact*. Marks are awarded for explaining how prevention measures reduce risk, not for naming controls in isolation.
