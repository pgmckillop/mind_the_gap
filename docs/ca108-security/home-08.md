# Content Area 8: Security

Students will develop knowledge and understanding of security within digital software development, including the types of information held by organisations and the reasons confidentiality, privacy and integrity must be maintained.

Students will examine security risks, threats and vulnerabilities affecting systems, data and individuals, including technical, human and physical factors. This includes understanding how failures in security can impact organisations through financial loss, legal action, reputational damage and reduced trust.

Students will explore common threat mitigation techniques and security controls, considering their purposes, processes, benefits and drawbacks. This includes methods used to prevent, detect and respond to security incidents, as well as procedures that support secure operation of digital systems.

Students will also examine how security measures are implemented and managed across digital environments, making judgements about the suitability of different approaches based on risk, effectiveness and organisational needs within digital software development.
