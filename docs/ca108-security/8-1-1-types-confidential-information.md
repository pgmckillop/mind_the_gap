# 8.1.1 

**Know the type of confidential information held by organisations**

This section covers the main categories of confidential information held by organisations, including Human Resources data, commercially sensitive information, and access information.

---

## Human Resources

Includes:

- salaries and benefits  
- staff personal details  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Relates directly to employees and internal workforce management  
- Contains personally identifiable and employment-specific records  
- Used to support payroll, contracts, performance management and legal compliance  

### Benefits

- Enables accurate administration of pay and benefits  
- Supports workforce planning and staff management  
- Provides evidence for contractual and regulatory obligations  

### Drawbacks

- High privacy risk if exposed or mishandled  
- Requires ongoing management and protection controls  
- Can lead to legal and reputational consequences if compromised  

---

## Commercially sensitive information

Includes:

- client details  
- stakeholder details  
- intellectual property  
- sales numbers  
- contracts  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Relates to business operations, relationships and competitive position  
- Often central to revenue generation and organisational strategy  
- May include proprietary or legally protected material  

### Benefits

- Supports commercial decision-making and planning  
- Protects competitive advantage  
- Enables continuity of client and stakeholder relationships  

### Drawbacks

- Exposure can weaken market position  
- Loss or leakage may result in financial impact  
- Requires controlled access and ongoing governance  

---

## Access information

Includes:

- usernames  
- passwords  
- multi-factor authentication (MFA) details  
- personal identification number (PIN)  
- access codes  
- passphrases  
- biometric data  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Used to identify, authenticate and authorise users  
- Controls entry to systems, data and services  
- Forms the foundation of digital security mechanisms  

### Benefits

- Enables secure system access  
- Supports accountability through user identification  
- Reduces risk of unauthorised activity when managed correctly  

### Drawbacks

- High-value target for attackers  
- Compromise can expose multiple systems simultaneously  
- Biometric data cannot be changed once leaked  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of how confidential information is managed depends on:

i. the sensitivity and volume of data held  
ii. the effectiveness of access controls and authentication methods  
iii. organisational constraints such as cost, staffing and technical capability  

Human Resources data prioritises privacy and compliance, commercially sensitive information focuses on protecting business value, and access information underpins system security. Each category carries different risk profiles, requiring proportionate protection measures based on impact, availability requirements and management overhead.
