# 8.1.3 


**Understand the potential impact to an organisation of failing to maintain privacy and confidentiality**

This section covers the consequences for organisations when privacy and confidentiality are not maintained, including regulatory, financial, reputational and security impacts.

---

## Non-compliance with regulations

Includes:

- loss of licence to practise  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Occurs when legal or regulatory requirements for data protection are breached  
- May result in enforced operational restrictions or removal of trading rights  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Loss of ability to operate legally  
- Immediate disruption to business activities  
- Long-term damage to organisational viability  

---

## Loss of trust

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Arises when clients, partners or staff lose confidence in how information is handled  
- Directly affects relationships and customer retention  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Reduced customer loyalty  
- Increased difficulty attracting new clients or partners  
- Internal morale may decline  

---

## Damage to organisation’s image

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Public perception of the organisation deteriorates following a breach  
- Often amplified through media or online platforms  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Long-term reputational harm  
- Reduced competitiveness  
- Negative impact on recruitment and partnerships  

---

## Financial loss

Includes:

- fines  
- refunds  
- loss of earnings/termination of contracts  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Direct monetary consequences arising from breaches  
- May include compensation payments and regulatory penalties  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Immediate financial strain  
- Reduced profitability  
- Potential loss of key contracts or revenue streams  

---

## Legal action

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Claims brought by individuals, clients or regulators  
- May involve court proceedings or settlements  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Legal costs and compensation payments  
- Management time diverted from core operations  
- Increased regulatory scrutiny  

---

## Reduced security

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Breaches weaken overall system protection  
- May expose further data or systems to compromise  

### Benefits

- None for the organisation  

### Drawbacks

- Increased vulnerability to future attacks  
- Greater risk of repeated incidents  
- Higher recovery and remediation costs  

---

## Judgement

Impact severity depends on:

i. the scale and sensitivity of the data involved  
ii. regulatory obligations and enforcement thresholds  
iii. organisational constraints such as budget, technical capability and incident response capacity  

Financial loss, reputational damage and reduced security often occur together, compounding overall impact. Regulatory non-compliance and legal action introduce additional operational risk, while loss of trust can have lasting effects on sustainability. Suitability of protection measures must balance cost and complexity against the potential scale of organisational harm.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing impacts without explanation. Link each consequence to its organisational effect (for example: breach → loss of trust → reduced revenue). Marks are awarded for showing how privacy failures lead to regulatory, financial, reputational and security outcomes.
