## Understand why information must be kept confidential by organisations

This section explains why organisations must protect different types of confidential information, focusing on competitive advantage, privacy, operational security and business continuity.

---

## Salaries and benefits

Includes:

- preventing competitors from offering higher wages to attract staff  
- preventing employees from comparing salaries or demanding comparable pay  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Directly linked to workforce retention and internal organisational stability  
- Forms part of Human Resources and contractual employment records  

### Benefits

- Reduces risk of staff poaching by competitors  
- Supports controlled workforce management  
- Helps maintain internal equity structures  

### Drawbacks

- Requires secure storage and restricted access  
- Mishandling can lead to employee dissatisfaction or legal consequences  

---

## Staff details

Includes:

- protecting privacy  
- preventing competitors from directly contacting employees  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Contains personally identifiable information  
- Used for communication, administration and employment management  

### Benefits

- Preserves individual privacy  
- Reduces risk of external interference with workforce stability  

### Drawbacks

- Exposure can lead to reputational damage  
- May result in regulatory or legal action if breached  

---

## Intellectual property

Includes:

- preventing competitors from copying designs  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Represents proprietary organisational knowledge and assets  
- Supports innovation and competitive positioning  

### Benefits

- Protects unique products, services and processes  
- Maintains commercial advantage  

### Drawbacks

- Requires ongoing protection mechanisms  
- Loss can permanently reduce organisational value  

---

## Client details

Includes:

- preventing competitors from contacting clients  
- protecting client privacy  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Covers personal and business-related client information  
- Central to customer relationships and service delivery  

### Benefits

- Preserves trust between organisation and client  
- Prevents loss of business to competitors  

### Drawbacks

- Breaches can lead to loss of confidence and customers  
- Increased compliance and management overhead  

---

## Sales numbers

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Reflect organisational performance and commercial position  
- Used for strategic planning and forecasting  

### Benefits

- Prevents competitors gaining insight into market strength  
- Protects negotiating position  

### Drawbacks

- Leakage may weaken competitive standing  
- Requires controlled internal access  

---

## Access information

Includes:

- preventing unauthorised access  

### Features / Characteristics / Role

- Enables identification, authentication and authorisation  
- Controls entry to systems and data  

### Benefits

- Protects digital systems from unauthorised use  
- Supports accountability and traceability  

### Drawbacks

- Compromise can expose multiple systems  
- Requires continual management and user compliance  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of confidentiality controls depends on:

i. the potential impact of disclosure on organisational operations and reputation  
ii. the sensitivity and volume of information held  
iii. constraints such as cost, technical capability and staff expertise  

Salaries, staff data and client details prioritise privacy and workforce stability, intellectual property protects competitive advantage, sales numbers affect strategic positioning, and access information underpins overall system security. Each category requires proportionate protection based on business risk and management overhead.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Avoid listing reasons in isolation. Link each information type to its organisational impact (for example: workforce stability, competitive advantage, privacy, or system security). Marks are awarded for connecting confidentiality → consequence → suitability, not for simple recall.
