## 1.2.3 Benefits and drawbacks of expressing algorithms in flowcharts

Flowcharts express algorithms using visual symbols to represent sequence, decisions, inputs/outputs and processes. They provide a graphical representation of algorithm structure and logic. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

### Benefits of using flowcharts

- provide a clear visual overview of algorithm structure  
- make logical flow and decision points easy to identify  
- support communication between technical and non-technical audiences  
- help identify missing steps or logical errors early in design  
- support step-by-step tracing of execution paths.

### Drawbacks of using flowcharts

- can become large and difficult to follow for complex algorithms  
- require time to construct and maintain  
- limited ability to represent detailed processing logic  
- changes to algorithms often require diagrams to be redrawn.

## Exam Focus
You should be able to:
- Explain how flowcharts represent algorithms
- Identify benefits of using flowcharts
- Identify drawbacks of using flowcharts
- Compare flowcharts with other algorithm representations
- Justify when flowcharts are suitable for expressing algorithms

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Questions may require judgement of flowchart suitability. Responses should weigh clarity and visual structure against scalability and maintenance when comparing flowcharts to other representation methods.
