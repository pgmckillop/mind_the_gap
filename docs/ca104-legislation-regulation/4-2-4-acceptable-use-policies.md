## Acceptable use policies (AUP)

This section covers the purpose of acceptable use policies and their typical content, including permitted and prohibited activities, working practices, communication etiquette and sanctions.

---

## Purpose of acceptable use policies (AUP)

**Features / Characteristics**
- Formal organisational documents defining acceptable behaviour when using digital systems  
- Apply to staff, contractors and users of organisational resources  

**Benefits**
- Sets clear expectations  
- Reduces misuse of systems  
- Supports legal and regulatory compliance  
- Protects organisational reputation  

**Drawbacks**
- Requires regular review to remain relevant  
- Enforcement creates administrative overhead  
- May be ignored without effective communication  

---

## Typical content

### Permitted activities

**Features / Characteristics**
- Defines approved use of systems, networks and data  

**Benefits**
- Clarifies acceptable behaviour  
- Supports consistent system usage  

**Drawbacks**
- May limit flexibility  
- Can become outdated as systems evolve  

---

### Prohibited activities

**Features / Characteristics**
- Identifies actions that are not allowed, such as unauthorised access or misuse  

**Benefits**
- Reduces risk of security breaches  
- Supports disciplinary action where required  

**Drawbacks**
- Requires monitoring and enforcement  
- Interpretation may vary between users  

---

### Working practices including confidentiality

**Features / Characteristics**
- Guidance on handling sensitive information and maintaining privacy  

**Benefits**
- Protects organisational and personal data  
- Builds trust with stakeholders  

**Drawbacks**
- Relies on user compliance  
- Additional training requirements  

---

### Communication etiquette including projecting correct organisation image

**Features / Characteristics**
- Standards for professional digital communication  

**Benefits**
- Maintains organisational reputation  
- Encourages respectful and appropriate interactions  

**Drawbacks**
- Subjective interpretation  
- Requires ongoing reinforcement  

---

### Sanctions/penalties

**Features / Characteristics**
- Defines consequences for policy breaches  

**Benefits**
- Reinforces accountability  
- Acts as a deterrent to misuse  

**Drawbacks**
- Can affect morale if applied inconsistently  
- Requires clear documentation and management oversight  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of acceptable use policies depends on:

i. clarity and accessibility of policy content  
ii. organisational capacity to enforce compliance  
iii. effectiveness of staff training and awareness  
iv. alignment with legal and security requirements  

Acceptable use policies support secure and professional use of digital systems but rely on consistent enforcement and user understanding. Their effectiveness is constrained by organisational resources, communication and monitoring capability.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
Marks are awarded for linking **AUP purpose → user behaviour → organisational impact**. Avoid listing policy items without explanation. In Judgement, reference constraints such as enforcement effort, training and consistency when evaluating suitability.
