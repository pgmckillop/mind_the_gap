## Health and safety legislation and implications for employers

This section covers the key points of relevant health and safety legislation and the implications for employers, including general workplace duties and display screen equipment requirements.

---

## Health and Safety at Work Act

### Key points

#### Provide a safe working environment

**Features / Characteristics**
- Employers must identify hazards and control risks  
- Workspaces must be suitable for the tasks being carried out  

**Benefits**
- Reduced workplace accidents  
- Improved staff wellbeing  
- Increased productivity through safer conditions  

**Drawbacks**
- Ongoing cost of risk management  
- Time required to maintain safe environments  

---

#### Ensure staff are properly trained

**Features / Characteristics**
- Training provided for equipment use, procedures and safety practices  

**Benefits**
- Reduced likelihood of incidents  
- Improved competence and confidence  

**Drawbacks**
- Training requires time and financial investment  
- Skills may require regular refresh  

---

#### Adequate welfare provision

**Features / Characteristics**
- Provision of suitable facilities and working conditions  

**Benefits**
- Supports employee health  
- Improves morale and retention  

**Drawbacks**
- Infrastructure and maintenance costs  

---

#### Provide relevant information, instruction and supervision

**Features / Characteristics**
- Clear communication of safety procedures  
- Ongoing oversight of working practices  

**Benefits**
- Improved compliance  
- Early identification of unsafe behaviour  

**Drawbacks**
- Management overhead  
- Resource demands for supervision  

---

## Display screen equipment (DSE)

### Implications for employers

#### Conduct a display screen equipment workstation assessment

**Features / Characteristics**

- Evaluation of posture, seating, screen position and workspace layout  

**Benefits**

- Reduces musculoskeletal and visual strain  
- Supports long-term employee health  

**Drawbacks**

- Requires time to assess and review workstations  
- May involve equipment replacement  

---

#### Reduce risks including making sure workers take breaks from display screen equipment work

**Features / Characteristics**

- Scheduled breaks and task variation  

**Benefits**

- Reduced fatigue  
- Improved concentration  

**Drawbacks**

- Potential short-term reduction in output  
- Requires monitoring and enforcement  

---

#### Provide an eye test if an employee asks for one

**Features / Characteristics**

- Access to eyesight testing related to screen use  

**Benefits**

- Early detection of vision problems  
- Reduced discomfort and errors  

**Drawbacks**

- Financial cost to employers  
- Administrative overhead  

---

#### Provide training and information for employees

**Features / Characteristics**

- Guidance on safe screen use and posture  

**Benefits**

- Increased awareness of health risks  
- Improved safe working practices  

**Drawbacks**

- Requires regular updates  
- Time commitment for delivery  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of employer health and safety measures depends on:

i. organisational capacity to fund training, assessments and welfare provision  
ii. availability of staff and management time to supervise compliance  
iii. scale of display screen equipment use  
iv. effectiveness of risk assessment processes  

Health and safety legislation supports safer working environments and improved employee wellbeing. However, implementation is constrained by cost, operational demands and the need for ongoing monitoring. Employers must balance legal compliance with practical resource management.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
    Marks are awarded for linking **legal duties → employer actions → impact on staff and organisation**. Avoid listing requirements without explaining why they matter. In Judgement, reference constraints such as cost, time and scale of DSE use to justify suitability.
