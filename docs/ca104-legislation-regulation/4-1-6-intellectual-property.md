# 4.1.6 Understand intellectual property legislation

This section covers intellectual property legislation, focusing on unregistered designs, registered designs and patents, and their role in protecting digital and technical creations.

---

## Unregistered designs

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- provide automatic protection for original designs without formal registration  
- protect the shape, configuration and appearance of products  
- offer limited duration of protection compared to registered rights  
- rely on the creator proving ownership and originality  

**Benefits**

- no registration cost or application process  
- immediate protection once a design is created  
- suitable for early-stage or rapidly evolving designs  

**Drawbacks**

- weaker legal protection  
- harder to enforce ownership  
- shorter protection period  
- increased risk of copying  

---

## Registered designs

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- formally registered intellectual property rights  
- protect visual appearance, including shape, pattern and configuration  
- provide documented ownership  
- offer stronger legal standing in disputes  

**Benefits**

- clearer proof of ownership  
- stronger enforcement rights  
- longer protection period  
- increased commercial value  

**Drawbacks**

- registration fees apply  
- application process requires time and administration  
- protection limited to approved registered features  

---

## Patents

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- protect new inventions and technical solutions  
- require formal application and examination  
- grant exclusive rights for a defined period  
- cover functionality rather than appearance  

**Benefits**

- strong legal protection for innovation  
- supports commercial exploitation  
- prevents competitors from using patented inventions  

**Drawbacks**

- expensive and time-consuming to obtain  
- public disclosure of technical details  
- strict eligibility requirements  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of intellectual property protection in digital software development depends on:

i. cost and time available for registration or application  
ii. commercial value of the design or invention  
iii. required strength of legal protection  
iv. organisational capability to manage enforcement  

Unregistered designs offer rapid but limited protection, registered designs provide clearer ownership, and patents deliver strong control over inventions. Effectiveness is constrained by financial resources, administrative capacity and the strategic importance of the intellectual asset.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
Do not list IP types without comparison. Link protection type → strength of rights → cost and enforcement. Higher marks come from explaining suitability based on commercial value, time and legal protection rather than simple definition.
