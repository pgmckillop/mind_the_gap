## Health and safety risks and preventative measures when working with digital systems

This section covers the health and safety risks associated with working with digital systems and the preventative measures used to mitigate those risks.

---

## Possible risks

### Using display screen equipment

**Features / Characteristics**
- Prolonged screen use  
- Fixed or poor posture during digital tasks  

**Benefits**
- Enables digital work and information processing  

**Drawbacks**
- Eye strain  
- Musculoskeletal discomfort  
- Fatigue and reduced concentration  

---

### Health and safety requirements

**Features / Characteristics**
- Obligation to follow workplace safety procedures  
- Need to manage physical and environmental risks  

**Benefits**
- Promotes consistent safety standards  
- Reduces likelihood of accidents  

**Drawbacks**
- Requires ongoing compliance monitoring  
- May introduce operational constraints  

---

## Methods of mitigating risk

### Adequate training

**Features / Characteristics**
- Instruction on safe equipment use and working practices  

**Benefits**
- Increased awareness of hazards  
- Reduced likelihood of injury  
- Improved confidence when using digital systems  

**Drawbacks**
- Time and financial cost  
- Training requires regular updates  

---

### Safe working environment

**Features / Characteristics**
- Appropriate workstation layout, lighting and ventilation  

**Benefits**
- Supports physical wellbeing  
- Reduces risk of accidents  

**Drawbacks**
- Infrastructure and maintenance costs  
- May require equipment upgrades  

---

### Safe working practices

**Features / Characteristics**
- Adoption of correct posture, breaks from screen use and task variation  

**Benefits**
- Reduced strain and fatigue  
- Improved long-term health  

**Drawbacks**
- Requires staff compliance  
- Potential short-term productivity impact  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of health and safety measures depends on:

i. availability of organisational resources for training and environment setup  
ii. effectiveness of staff engagement with safe practices  
iii. scale of digital system use  
iv. management capacity to monitor compliance  

Risk mitigation improves wellbeing and reduces workplace incidents, but effectiveness is constrained by cost, time and behavioural factors. Balanced implementation supports safety while maintaining operational efficiency.

---

!!! tip "Exam tip"
Link **risk → preventative measure → impact on staff and organisation**. Avoid listing hazards or controls without explanation. Judgement must balance safety benefits against constraints such as cost, time and staff compliance.
