# Content Area 4: Legislation andd Regulation

Students will develop knowledge and understanding of legislation and regulatory requirements relevant to digital software development, including health and safety, data protection, computer misuse, equality and intellectual property.

Students will examine how legal requirements affect organisations and individuals, recognising employer responsibilities, employee obligations and the consequences of non-compliance. This includes understanding health and safety risks associated with digital systems, data security and protection responsibilities, and the impact of computer misuse.

Students will also explore professional guidelines, codes of conduct and digital industry standards, considering how these influence professional behaviour, quality of work and ethical practice. The importance of acceptable use policies and whistleblowing procedures will be addressed.

Judgements will be made about the interrelationships between digital software development, legislation and guidelines, and their impact on organisations, society and individuals within a digital context.
