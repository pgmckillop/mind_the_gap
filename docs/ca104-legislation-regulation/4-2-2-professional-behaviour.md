# 4.2.2 Understand how guidelines in codes of conduct influence professional behaviour

This section covers how guidelines in codes of conduct influence professional behaviour, including compliance with policies and legislation, quality of work, meeting deadlines, effective communication, and maintaining confidentiality and trust.

---

## Ensuring individuals follow policies, procedures and legislation

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- provide formal behavioural expectations aligned with organisational rules and legal requirements  
- define acceptable and unacceptable professional conduct  
- support consistent application of policies and procedures  

**Benefits**

- improves compliance with legal and organisational requirements  
- reduces risk of misconduct  
- supports accountability across teams  

**Drawbacks**

- requires monitoring and enforcement  
- may increase administrative overhead  
- effectiveness depends on staff awareness and engagement  

---

## Ensuring quality of work

### Minimising risk to the public  
### Acting with competence and integrity  

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- require professionals to apply appropriate skills and knowledge  
- mandate ethical decision making  
- emphasise responsibility for the impact of digital systems on users and society  

**Benefits**

- reduces likelihood of harm caused by poor design or implementation  
- promotes reliable and safe digital services  
- strengthens professional credibility  

**Drawbacks**

- demands ongoing training and development  
- increases organisational responsibility for quality assurance  

---

## Meeting deadlines

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- establish expectations for timely delivery of work  
- support coordination across projects and stakeholders  

**Benefits**

- improves project reliability  
- supports effective planning and resource allocation  

**Drawbacks**

- deadline pressure may affect quality if poorly managed  
- requires careful balancing of speed and accuracy  

---

## Effective communication

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- promotes clear, accurate and appropriate information sharing  
- supports collaboration with technical and non-technical stakeholders  

**Benefits**

- reduces misunderstanding  
- improves teamwork and project outcomes  
- supports transparent decision making  

**Drawbacks**

- requires communication skills training  
- miscommunication can still occur despite guidelines  

---

## Maintaining confidentiality and trust

**Features / Characteristics / Role**

- requires protection of sensitive information  
- reinforces ethical handling of data and intellectual property  
- supports professional relationships  

**Benefits**

- strengthens client and stakeholder confidence  
- reduces risk of data breaches  
- supports organisational reputation  

**Drawbacks**

- requires strict access controls and governance  
- breaches can have significant legal and reputational impact  

---

## Judgement

Suitability of codes of conduct in guiding professional behaviour depends on:

i. clarity and accessibility of guidelines  
ii. organisational commitment to enforcement  
iii. staff competence and training provision  
iv. availability of resources to support quality assurance and communication  

Codes of conduct provide structured expectations for professional practice, but their effectiveness is constrained by implementation, monitoring and organisational culture.

---

Avoid listing behaviours without impact. Link guidelines → professional actions → organisational outcomes such as quality, risk reduction and trust. Higher marks come from explaining suitability in terms of compliance, competence and resource constraints.
